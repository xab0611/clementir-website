OUR STORY

More than bread. A place at the table.

Hapag Bakehouse is a fictional family-run neighborhood bakery. It began as a home-based bakery in 2019 and moved into a small storefront in 2022.

Inspired by the Filipino idea of hapag — the dining table — our story is about bringing people together through everyday bread, sweet treats, and pastries made for life's celebrations.
